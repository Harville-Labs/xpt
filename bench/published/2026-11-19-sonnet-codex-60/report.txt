Rule: tokens, tools, and wall s show the variant's median as a change from the baseline's with a 95% bootstrap interval; solved, wrong, and weak show the difference in rates with a 95% Newcombe-Wilson interval. A metric names the better arm only when its interval excludes no change, and is a tie otherwise. The verdict is a win only when the token interval lies wholly below no change, a loss when it lies wholly above, and a tie otherwise. Quality is a guard: a lower solve rate or more wrong accepts or weakened tests is a loss even inside the interval. The all row is the geometric mean of the per-task ratios, resampled within each task, not a pool of raw runs.
Resolution: at 5 runs per arm the median per-task token interval is about ±24%. Resolving a ±10% token change needs about 25 runs per arm per task.
